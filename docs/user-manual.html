1. What this system does

The School Management System is a web portal for Ghanaian basic schools. It keeps one employment file per staff member, one admission file per pupil, daily attendance, two-step leave approval, monthly salary runs, and an explainable anomaly desk that blocks payment when something looks wrong.

  • Staff, students, parent emails, attendance, class-teacher assignment, and leave (HR then Headteacher).
  • Payroll using 5.5% employee SSNIT, staff loans and unpaid absence. PAYE is not deducted.
  • Automatic scan after every payroll run, with evidence, risk score, narrative and a recommended action.
  • A draft cannot be approved or marked paid while critical anomalies are still open.
Open the app at http://localhost:5173 after starting the API (php artisan serve in backend) and the UI (npm run dev in frontend). On Codespaces use forwarded port 5173.

5. Dashboard and navigation

After sign-in you land on the operations dashboard. It greets you by time of day and shows school health for payroll.

  • Hold payroll — one or more critical flags are still open. Do not approve salaries.
  • Review recommended — open flags remain, but they are not critical.
  • Clear to proceed — no open payroll flags.

Campus slides and shortcuts take you to classes, staff, students, parents, attendance, leave, payroll, anomalies, reports and audit, according to your role. Click a KPI card to open the related desk.

15. Leave approval

Path: Leave. Filter: All, Awaiting HR, Awaiting Headteacher, Approved, Rejected.

  1. Staff (or HR on their behalf) submit the form. Status: Awaiting HR.
  2. HR forwards or rejects. Forwarded status: Awaiting Headteacher.
  3. Headteacher approves or rejects. Approved leave suppresses the “paid without attendance” flag for that period.

16. Prepare monthly payroll form

HR only Path: Payroll → Prepare payroll.

Section A — Pay period
FieldRequiredNotes
First day of periodRequiredChoosing a date fills last day and run name for that calendar month
Last day of periodRequiredYou may still edit it
Payment dateRequiredPay day
Run nameRequirede.g. August 2026 staff payroll

Section B explains what the run will do:

  • Earnings — basic salary plus posted allowances for every active staff member.
  • SSNIT employee 5.5% — first-tier on capped insurable basic (2026 cap GHS 69,000). Employer 13% is recorded, not deducted from net.
  • Loans and absence — active staff-loan recovery and unpaid / absent days after approved leave.
  • No PAYE — income tax is not taken. Net = gross − SSNIT − loans − absence.

Tick: Attendance and approved leave for this period have been closed. The anomaly scan will run automatically. Then Generate salary run.

18. Anomaly investigation form

Path: Anomalies then open a case. Filter by status (Open, Acknowledged, Resolved, False positive, All), severity, rule, run and search.

Each case shows what fired, recommended action, staff on the flag, evidence, related cases, risk score, confidence, and whether the case blocks approval.

Fix actions (left of the case)

  • HR — edit the staff file, deactivate a leaver, or open attendance.
  • Payroll — recalculate this slip from the current file, or remove the person from this draft.
  • Removing someone from a draft closes their flags so the Headteacher can approve.

Investigation outcome form

Payroll Headteacher Auditor Accountants can read only. HR corrects the file; Payroll records the outcome.

22. Payroll rules and detection catalogue

The engine is rule-based, not a trained model, so every flag can be explained to a Headteacher and an auditor. A scan runs after every generate, recalculate or related change.

CodeWhat it catchesTypical fix
Ghost_No_User_AccountSalary without a linked user accountHR links a login; Payroll recalculates
Ghost_Inactive_StaffInactive employee still on the runConfirm status or remove from draft
Payment_Without_AttendancePay with no attendance (approved leave suppresses this)Mark the register or drop the line
Duplicate_Period_PaymentTwo payments for the same periodRemove the extra salary line
Unusually_High_SalaryGross above 1.5× grade/basicCorrect salary/grade; recalculate
Ssnit_MismatchEmployee SSNIT not 5.5% of capped basicCorrect salary; recalculate
Bank_Details_MissingMissing bank name or accountCapture banking or hold the person
Loan_Over_DeductionDeduction above outstanding loanCap recovery; recalculate
Unauthorized_AllowanceUnauthorised or over-cap allowanceRemove or authorize; recalculate

Ghana config lives in backend payroll settings: employee SSNIT 5.5%, employer 13%, 2026 insurable cap GHS 69,000. School-level payroll does not deduct PAYE.

23. Typical monthly workflow

  1. HR keeps staff files current (salary, bank, status, SSNIT). Teachers mark class attendance; HR/Headteacher close the staff register.
  2. Staff request leave. HR forwards; Headteacher approves before pay day.
  3. HR prepares the month, confirms attendance and leave are closed, generates the run.
  4. Open Anomalies. Work every critical case: fix the file, recalculate, or remove from draft. Save an outcome with notes.
  5. Headteacher opens the run. If critical flags remain, approval is blocked. When clear, Approve for payment.
  6. HR Mark paid and download payslips. Accountant uses Reports for SSNIT and net totals. Auditor uses Audit trail.

24. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
Login failed (staff)Use Staff ID tab, not Email. Match the generated ID and employment email exactly. Staff have no password.
Login failed (officer)Email tab plus password. Demo password is password. Parents must be registered by HR first.
Cannot register staffSign in as HR. First name, surname, email, staff ID and basic salary are required.
Cannot prepare payrollOnly HR can. Tick the attendance/leave closed box.
Cannot approve payrollOpen critical flags. Resolve them or remove the people from the draft.
Leave rejected by the formCheck remaining days, max per request, working vs calendar days, and the declaration checkbox.
My class is emptyAsk Headteacher or HR to assign you as class tutor under Classes.
Cannot edit assessmentsOnly the assigned class tutor can save scores.
Parent did not get mailDemo writes to the API log unless SMTP is set. They still see in-app notices after sign-in.
Menu item missingYour desk does not include that module. See the roles table in chapter 3.
